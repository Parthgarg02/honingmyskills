Apple Pay is finally in India. It went live on 30 September 2026, about 12 years after it launched in the US.

It's been a long wait, and the RBI's rules were a big part of it. In 2021 the RBI's card tokenisation rules came in. Apple stopped taking credit and debit cards for App Store and iCloud purchases in India around that time, and card payments only started coming back this July.

After a lot of conversations and regulatory compliance, Apple Pay is now here. But only with Axis Bank for now.

## What works right now

- Axis Bank credit cards on Visa and Mastercard
- Payments in shops, in apps and on websites, from iPhone, Apple Watch and iPad (Mac support is coming soon)

## What doesn't work yet

- RuPay cards
- Debit cards
- UPI
- Other banks. HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and SBI Card are not part of the launch, and reports say they are still negotiating commercial terms with Apple.

## My experience so far

I've been using it, and it works well. I've paid with it on Swiggy and Zepto, and the experience was seamless. I also use it to tap on POS machines.

Let's see how it goes from here.
