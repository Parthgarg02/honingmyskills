Well, Board Games do have some limitations right? Board Games help us in our mental workout but what about our physical workout?

Now, I think everyone should do any kind of activity to keep their body active throughout their lives. So here is my another interest - Tennis. It is one of the fastest sports. I just love this sport, the types of shots we can play, the choices we get.

![Two yellow Australian Open tennis balls resting side by side](/images/posts/active-body-active-mind-1.webp)

Let me tell you something about tennis, its the only sports game where a player may have won lesser points than his/her opponent and still be the winner of the match. Do comment if you want to know how is that possible, but I can assure you it certainly is.

So, new year has started and with that new set of Grand Slam has also begun. Australian Open is on its verge, and I am very excited to see who will reach the top of the ladder this time. Australian Open has always been held in Melbourne from 3rd to 22nd February.

Though I honor every player who is out there, participating in this Grand Slam, but as we all know it’s human nature to feel a connection with some specific player, a connection with their playing style, or whatever the reason might be. Then how can I savor myself from this basic human nature? So, here I am feeling a connection with an Australian Player Dominic Thiem, an Argentine player Diego Schwartzman, an American player Sofia Kenin, & Japanese player Naomi Osaka, whom I will be supporting throughout the Australian Open.

In case you happen to like Tennis as well, then do let me know which player are you supporting.

Actually, I have started watching Tennis during the lockdown, as I was unable to play myself, so I had to satisfy myself by watching it on TV (to be honest I was forced to watch by one of my friends), and I am glad that I was forced as I have learned a lot by just watching them play, their control over the ball, their swing, their serve, just mind-blowing.
