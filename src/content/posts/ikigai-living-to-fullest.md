During lockdown i read a book named “[Ikigai](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/33357041-ikigai)”. Ikigai is a Japanese concept of living to fullest. Though i do not follow everything i have read, but i followed some of the things, like getting up early in the morning.

![The book Ikigai: The Japanese Secret to a Long and Happy Life, standing on a wooden tray](/images/posts/ikigai-living-to-fullest-1.webp)

Trust me when i say i used to get up at 10 in the morning and not before that, and now that has completely changed, i have started getting up early, during summers i got up at 5:30, and during winters i am getting up at 6:45.

Their basic concept for getting up early was that to do something that excites you, or is your favourite, or a thing of which you dont get bored to do in the morning. It can be anything like Music, cycling, any activity with friends, or literally anything.

Just give it a try for like 1 week and you will love this idea, it is as simple as that. I know at first you wont get up, snooze your alarm like 5-6 times and then finally turn the alarm off and go back to sleep, its common. But once you are determined to atleast give it a try, you will eventually come up with a plan. If i can do it, then you can do it.

Initially i started to go out for cycling alone. My first day it felt as if i had achieved some kind of goal or something. Then i met with some of my old friends, who used to go for cycling at that time, so i started to go out with them, they pushed me to do more. After a week we started to go on for long rides, they even made me go for like 50 Km and that to just after like 2 weeks of cycling.

Now i go for tennis and some days even cycling or running with my brothers, it is so much fun to do all that and still getting back home before 10 A.M.

At last i would recommend anyone of you to atleast give [Ikigai](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/33357041-ikigai) a try, you may not get up early, but you may take up any other good habit from it.

And yes, please do join me in my morning routines if you are in Lucknow and want to start something like this.
