During lockdown I started something which I was planning to start from a very long time, Photography. To understand the fundamentals of Photography I did what everybody does now a days, which is YouTube.

So I started to watch video after video just to learn the fundamentals of Photography that are:

- Aperture – It defines the light entering through your lens,
- Shutter Speed – It defines length/time the shutter remains open, which again defines the light exposing on the sensors and
- ISO – Which again helps our digital camera in capturing light.

So as you can see all the three fundamentals help our camera’s ability to capture light but all three of them work in their own different ways and are used for different kinds of photography.

If you want to start photography, don’t set your whole Camera to manual mode. Instead I would recommend you to either control Aperture or Shutter speed at the beginning.

Try to spend some months exploring that one mode only, you will be shocked by the amount of experience you can get from controlling just one mode, you can even spend months or even years on it and still there will be room to learn.
