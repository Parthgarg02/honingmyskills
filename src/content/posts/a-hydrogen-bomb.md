Do you guys know, the sun which we see is actually not on fire. In fact, the Sun does not use any Oxygen, which is necessary for fire and hence can never be on fire.

Sun is not a ball of fire, rather it is a ball of gas, a Hydrogen Bomb instead. Just like an electric bulb is hot and bright but nothing is on fire, in the same way, the Sun is not and bright but nothing is on fire.

![The Sun glowing blue in an ultraviolet image, with bright flares along its edge](/images/posts/a-hydrogen-bomb-1.webp)

Every Second 700 Million tons of hydrogen gets converted into 695 Million tons of helium. When this happens, energy is released as gamma rays, which get converted to light.
