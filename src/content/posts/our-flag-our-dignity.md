Can anyone tell me why do we purchase our National Flag every year, why do we need another if we purchased it the year before?

![The Indian national flag flying against a blue sky with scattered clouds](/images/posts/our-flag-our-dignity-1.webp)

In my personal opinion, the selling and purchasing of the National Flag should be strictly-prohibited. Only authorized people should be able to purchase National Flags, and there must be some penalties for not keeping it according to the rules.

I see many people buy National Flag on 15th August and 26th January, and then not keeping it safe. Some people even give the flags to their children for whom it is just another toy, and they play with it till they get bored, after which they throw the flag on the ground and move on to the next toy. I don’t blame them it’s not their fault, they don’t even know the significance of the flag yet.

Think yourself, why do we have to buy flags every 15th August or 26th January if we bought them the year before as well? Why do we need another?

The answer is that we fail to keep them in safekeeping, or some will say extra safe that even they are not able to find, or you gave it to someone. Trust me when I say that I have seen our National Flag lying on the road and many drivers driving their cars over it. My heart sank when I saw this for the first time.

National Flags are no joke, they represent our nation, there are rules for their safekeeping, and one of the rules is that the flag should never touch the ground. I guess we can at least oblige to this simple rule. And once we start following this rule, we will understand that it’s not easy to keep the flag always up high.

As everyone thinks differently, hence I cannot say that I am right or you are wrong. Please do understand that I respect everyone’s thought process and do not disrespect those who buy or sell flags. They are paying their respects to our country in their own ways. But I do emphasize you all to think upon this.

Jai Hind, Jai Bharat..
