**Ever had those “college thoughts” like, “Hey, what if, like, my red is, like, your blue?”**

A few months back I asked this weird question to my brother, of course at the moment my question seemed to be weird, but my brother also loves to talk about stuff that makes no sense to others.

The question simply means that what if everyone or a few of us perceive colors differently? That day my brother had no answer to it so we just laughed at that stupid question, but few days later my brother sent me a link to a study which is being conducted for the exact same question I had.

![Overlapping red, green and blue circles on black, mixing into yellow, magenta, cyan and white](/images/posts/perception-census-1.webp)

Link to that study is : [https://perceptioncensus.dreamachine.world/](https://perceptioncensus.dreamachine.world/home)

This question popped into my head as my brother is color blind and that day in the office we all were taking a color blindness test just for fun. So, of course, my brother being color blind did not perform well, but I noticed most of the other people as well had different answers to a few pictures. Most pictures had colors like red or green, where our answers differ.

And then this question popped into my head, that maybe everyone perceives colors differently, and maybe my red is like your blue and we don’t know that cause from childhood you call that color red. So an apple is red in color we all know that but few people might see it as red and few people might see it as blue or green or whatever, and at the same time we all call it red, as from childhood we are told that that color is red. Confusing right !!

By this, I end my thought here, for you to give it a thought. Do let me know what are your thoughts about it.
