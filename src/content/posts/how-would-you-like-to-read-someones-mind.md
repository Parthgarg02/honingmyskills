You must have heard a common saying that “the eyes are the windows to the soul”, or like my mom always say that “my eyes speak how I feel”. And I say that these sayings are all true.

And the best part about eyes is that even if people don’t want you to know how they feel or if they are lying, they still can’t change how their eyes behave.

![Two faces in profile, a man and a woman, looking closely at each other](/images/posts/how-would-you-like-to-read-someones-mind-1.webp)

So let me begin with how you can read someone’s mind without even them noticing. First you need to build some knowledge about that person’s usual behavior, so that later you can notice any change.

Of course, it’s easier to tap into a family or a friend’s mind than into a complete stranger, as you are well aware of their usual behavior, eye movement, or simple gestures. So you get a sense of how they are feeling, or if they are lying to you or not. But it’s also easier to develop the same knowledge in case of a complete stranger.

You just have to know how they normally talk, their normal reactions, or their normal behavior. To develop the sense of that you can talk to them about general stuff like weather, a match, or anything else by which they are not connected and look for their normal behavior, their eye movements, and their gestures. After which you can talk about the stuff you want them to answer, and know if they are lying, making up a story, or are not interested to talk further about it.

Few signals observed in people while talking:

- Looking up and right - they are lying or tapping into their imagination.
- Looking up and left - they are searching their memory and hence speaking the truth.
- Looking down and right - they are creating a feeling or memory, for example, thinking what it would be like to be in Paris.
- Looking up and left - they are talking to themselves.
- Looking towards right - tapping into their auditory thought, for example, remembering a song.
- Looking towards their left – Tapping into their visual thought, for example, remembering the color of a dress.

These few signals can help you detect if someone is lying or telling the truth.

Apart from the above-mentioned signals, widening of eyes or contraction of the eyes can also tell us a lot about how the person is feeling, or their reaction to a particular thing, or a reaction to what the other person is talking about.

The widening of pupils can tell us many things like our eyes widen when we are aroused or when we are processing any information. Our eyes even widen when we think there is any danger or we fear something. Our eyes also tend to be widening if we are choosing between two or more things. A study says that our pupils get bigger as choices get harder and harder.

While contraction of pupil tells us if the other person believes what we are saying or not or if he feels disgusted by it. It even shows how focused we are at that particular moment.

![Close-up of a pair of wide-open eyes](/images/posts/how-would-you-like-to-read-someones-mind-2.webp)

In the above image, left eye shows widened pupil or dilated pupil & the right eye shows contracted pupil, to give an example.

We can also detect what people like and what they do not like by following their gaze and watching If the eyes are widened or contracted. For eg., If their eyes are wide and they are gazing at something then they are either curious or they like that stuff. While if they are squinting while gazing then they do not like that or think it’s weird.

The context of the situation is very important as pupil size can mean many things. Pupil size only reflects the strength of feelings, rather than whether those feelings are positive or negative. Therefore, to deduce whether someone is feeling good or bad, we need to consider the context of the situation in addition to their eyes.

Though it might not be possible to read someone’s *exact* thoughts from just looking at their eyes. This is great because, from the perspective of the observed, the privacy of thoughts is maintained. But eyes tell us much more than we sometimes assume, and our eyes, unlike our mouths, cannot lie.

I would suggest practicing this first on your friends or family members, after which when you have developed this particular skill try on others. Slowly you will develop this skill in you sub-conscious mind, after which you wont even notice that you are using this skill.
