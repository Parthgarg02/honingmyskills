If you've checked this blog in the last couple of years, you found nothing new. No posts, no updates, just a quiet page.

I didn't forget about it. I was busy in a way I couldn't explain in a single post.

## It started with one exam.

In November 2023 I sat for the CA Finals. Months of preparation came down to a few papers, and then a result. When it came, I became **CA Parth Garg**. And that's just the beginning.

## Then came a question I couldn't ignore.

With the qualification in hand, I had safer options. I could have joined my father's business, or taken a job with a steady salary. But I kept coming back to one thought. What if I built something of my own?

So I started my own CA firm. That taught me things no exam prepares you for, like clients, trust, deadlines and responsibility.

## Then 2025 happened.

This is the part I haven't talked about until now. While this blog sat silent, I was building two more things.

The first is [Dazzlecrest](https://dazzlecrest.com), a D2C brand selling demi-fine and imitation jewellery online. The second is [GoTaxHub (GTH)](https://gotaxhub.com), a platform for businesses that need more than a tax filing. It covers compliance, plus business consultancy and strategy.

Both launched in 2025.

So the gap wasn't a break. It was a build phase: an exam, a firm, a brand and a platform.

## Why I'm back

This blog is called *Honing My Skills* for a reason. I've learned a lot in these years, and now I have real stories to tell. Coming up:

- Becoming a CA, and what the journey really looked like
- Starting a firm and a brand from scratch
- The tools behind the brand
- Moving from COD to fully prepaid orders, and then back to COD with adjustments
- Books that shaped how I think

Stay with me. The quiet years are over.
