Hi guys, so here is my other hobby which brings a lot of joy and laughter and at the same time can test your skills to think, presence of mind, and sometimes how far you can think - Board Games.

![A hand moving pieces on a colourful Ticket to Ride board game map](/images/posts/if-you-win-you-win-if-you-lose-1.webp)

Now Now, don’t directly jump to conclusions of Ludo or even Monopoly. I am talking about some high-level board games which force you to think out of the box, force you to test your presence of mind, force you to develop strategies that will sometimes apparently go wrong just after another players move and then again go around that.

Overall they tell the way you think, the way you approach things, and the way you deal with situations.

I feel that board games can come in handy to test yourself and to improve your abovementioned skills. They let you explore yourself and help you to figure out things in real life as well. You feel more confident about your strategies and they make you believe more in your instincts (If they work out obviously, but hey, don’t worry if they don’t in starting, just keep on playing with better players, and you will notice the change yourself).

The best part is if you play with good players you have to think differently every time you play. So just improving our skills further.

Here are some of the Board Games which I love playing:

- Power Grid (The above picture is of this game)
- Decrypto
- Modern Art
- Code Names
- Last Will

And the list goes on. It is an amazing experience one can get on his own. Do let me know your favorite board games in the comments section below, will be happy to try them my self.

In the end, I would just say there is nothing to lose. If you win, you win - if you lose, you get the opportunity to perform better the next time. So, ***NEVER LOSE HOPE.***
