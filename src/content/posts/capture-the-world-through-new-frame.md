So my family was planning a trip and though I am not a great photographer or something but still I was in a dilemma about which lens should I take for the trip. So again I started googling things & saw some YouTube videos to know which type of lens would be perfect to take on a trip, which can click decent portrait photos as well as nature photos.

![A wide-angle photo of an empty road curving past a park under a cloudy evening sky](/images/posts/capture-the-world-through-new-frame-1.webp)

Though I had some idea of lenses and what they are capable of, but when I googled them further then I got to know that this is a whole market in itself. First of all, there are 2 basic categories of Camera Lenses:

1. Prime Lenses - These lenses have a fixed focal length that means you can not zoom in or zoom out. But they are very fast and have sharper image quality. These are light in weight and hence easy to carry during trips.

2. Zoom Lenses - These lenses have a dynamic range of focal length making them more flexible to use. And they have more glass to aid dynamic focal length within one lens and hence make them heavier.

Now, we have many options available under these two categories as well.

1. Standard Lens - Or many people call them as walkaround lens, as these are the lens which most people use when they are not sure which lens they should use. These lenses have a good focal length of around 18mm - 85mm. These can be used in all sorts of situations, but may not be best in any. Though they can produce a sharper image than any other specific lens.

2. Macro Lens - These lenses are best for close-up shots. They are known for their sharp image and extremely close range. These lenses are great for nature photography.

3. Telephoto Lens - These are the type of lens every beginner must be wanting as they must be thinking this to be either cool or expensive. These lenses specialize in focusing on distant objects, mainly used where you can’t get too close to your subject for example during wildlife photography or sports photography or even while taking a shot of the moon as given below.

![The same moon shown through 100mm, 200mm, 400mm and 800mm lenses, growing larger with each](/images/posts/capture-the-world-through-new-frame-2.webp)

4. Wide Angle Lens - These lenses help you to capture a large area into your frame. These are useful while clicking landscape photography or while clicking a mountain range as well.

Before doing this research I wanted to go for Telephoto Lens, but then I thought that this will restrict most of my photos, and hence decided to replace my walkaround lens for a better one. Till now I have been using my kit lens only which was 18mm - 55mm f/3.5-5.6. But now I will be replacing it with 18mm - 140mm f/3.5-5.6. This will give me more focal length at a cost of some sharpness, while not restricting any of my shots. And the change in sharpness won’t be even noticeable.

So this was my journey towards buying my first Lens, do let me know yours.
