After becoming a Chartered Accountant, I had a choice to make.

## Two roads

One road led to a business that already existed: our family's jewellery store. On paper, it was the safer option. My father saw it differently. When you run a jewellery store, there are many threats, and that was his main concern. He was the one who didn't want me to continue it.

The other road led to nothing yet. No clients, no track record, only the decision to build something of my own.

I took the second one, and my father was happy about it. I started G A & Company with my cousin, CA Kanha Agarwal.

## Zero to one

I won't pretend it was easy. Building a practice from scratch is tough, and the hardest part was finding clients. In the beginning, we had none.

Then the first one came, through a reference from my sister's friend. One reference was where it started. After that, slowly and steadily, things began to fall into place, and the rest became history.

## Show up every morning

If I had to put what I learned in those early months into one line, it would be this: things take time to settle. Nobody tells you how long "slowly" is. You can't stop, and you have to stay consistent. You just show up every morning.

## There's another chapter

The family business I didn't step into is still moving ahead, through a brand I started: [Dazzlecrest](https://dazzlecrest.com). How I ended up there is the story for my next post.
