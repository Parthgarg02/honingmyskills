As someone who has always been curious about the World Wars and the political climate of that era, I’ve often tried to read up on the subject. However, I found the traditional sources of information to be dry and uninspiring, leading me to procrastinate endlessly.

But a few weeks ago I discovered a Historical Fiction book, Fall of Giants - by Ken Follett, centered around the First World War. I was immediately drawn into the story, and as I immersed myself in its pages, I found myself learning about the historical events and political movements of the time. The book was a fascinating and engaging way to explore the era.

![Book cover of Fall of Giants by Ken Follett](/images/posts/fall-of-giants-by-ken-follett-1.webp)

Fall of Giants is a captivating novel written by the renowned British author, Ken Follett. Published in 2010, it is the first book in his Century Trilogy. The book is set during the period of World War I, and it tells the story of five interrelated families from different parts of the world.

As the title suggests, Fall of Giants is a story about the downfall of great powers. The book explores themes of war, love, betrayal, and social change. It is a sweeping epic that covers a vast array of characters and settings. From the halls of power in London to the battlefields of France and the coal mines of Wales, the book takes the reader on a journey through a turbulent time in world history.

Fall of Giants is an outstanding work of historical fiction. Ken Follett has created a masterpiece that is both entertaining and informative. The character development is exceptional, the historical accuracy is impressive, and the writing is superb. It is a book that will stay with you long after you have finished reading it, and I would highly recommend it to anyone looking for a great & informative read.

I’m eagerly looking forward to reading the next installment in the series, Winter of the World. This book continues the saga of the families introduced in Fall of Giants, delving into the fascinating history of World War II and the political climate of the time. I can’t wait to see how Ken Follett weaves together the complex threads of this turbulent period in world history.
