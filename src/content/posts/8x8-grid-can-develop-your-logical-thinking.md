Have you ever played the game Chess. The game is highly underrated these days, but this game can challenge your brain each and every time you play. There is no one way of winning this game, as every time your opponent will also be thinking differently. There is no one solution to anything, as different minds can get you different solutions to different problems.

Now I would have to thank the famous TV series “The Queen’s Gambit” for bringing back Chess and making more and more people excited about Chess. So one day my brother called me up and asked me to play Chess with him. I played like few games with him, but since then i am playing like 3-4 games daily and i can see the difference.

A little history, you guys would love to know that the origins of this game are actually traced back to India. From where it spread to Persia, and when Arabs conquered Persia, Chess was taken up by the Muslim world and subsequently spread to Southern Europe. In Europe the game was evolved to its present form.

Now this game is actually a package of benefits, will surely cover them in my next post. But stating few of them here will be seeing through someone else’s perspective, can increase your IQ, improves memory, make you more creative and many more.
