Amrut Deshmukh is on a mission. His mission is to Make India Read. And hence created an app called Booklet. It’s an amazing app for people who do want to start reading books, but just don’t know how to start, which book to pick, or if wondering they won’t be able to read 200-300 pages.

![Promotional graphic for the BVG Booklet app, "Make India Read", with a phone showing book summaries](/images/posts/amrut-deshmukh-on-a-mission-1.webp)

I know as I was one of those about 2 years back. My uncle made me read just 2 books and asked every day what have I read the previous day. And after reading 2 books I used to tell him which book I want to read next.

The first book is always the hardest one to complete (Mine was Benjamin Franklin), but once you finish that, trust me that will change you. It has changed me my first book took almost 3 months to get over and there were only 200 pages. And now I have also finished books with 500 pages within 3 days.

So coming back to Booklet, the best thing about this app is that it is completely free. The summary is almost like just 2 pages, and even if you don’t want to read that you can just listen by downloading voice for the same.

At first, you can only download one book at a time so that you are not just wasting the resources you are getting, and also so that new readers don’t get confused with so many options given. After the app sees that you are a regular, then you will be able to download multiple books as well.

The only drawback to the whole process is that you can read only those books which the creator has already read and made the summary as well. But don’t worry the creator has read many books and has made a summary for the same as well.

But something is always better than nothing. I would recommend all of you to at least give it a try. It’s a great habit, you never know maybe you also become a book lover.
