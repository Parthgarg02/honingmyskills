Hi Guys, I started my website on 10th January, 2021. It was my brother who told me on a morning walk, that everyone should write personal blogs, it helps them personally & professionally. (He also has his own site [https://fully-faltoo.com/](https://fully-faltoo.com/) do check it out.) He also told me & has repeated this a lot of times that “***Internet is where Magic Happens***”.

![Black and white photo of two people's legs walking along a cobbled pavement](/images/posts/my-journey-towards-blogging-1.webp)

I didn’t ask much about it then, but when I got back home I texted him asking which site I should register at (I was not thinking to buy a domain at that time). And the first thing he recommended me was to get a domain name & then started suggesting some random names.

I told him that I will talk to him about this tomorrow in the office, to which he replied & I quote “***Inspiration is Perishable***, so you just tell any name, if you have in mind” to which I said Honing My Skills, & the very next moment he replied, “Purchased”.

So this was how I started my blogging site, or should I say this is how I was led to start my site.

I would also recommend any of you to start writing, write anything you want, but what’s most important is to write for yourself.

I will be posting some of the services I use for my website, so stay tuned if interested.
