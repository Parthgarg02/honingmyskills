So a few days ago my friend texted me this weird question (Mahabharat Effect), and this question actually made me think. I came up with some answers and called my friend, we discussed it, we may be partially right, but that towards the end of the blog.

So of course after discussing this matter I went to check this up on google, and I found some answers, and this is why I am here to share the same with you.

![A person walking on a road with a red and white cane](/images/posts/what-do-born-blind-people-see-in-dreams-1.webp)

Now when I googled it I found that scientists have done some research about this. And they claim that a born blind person’s eyes move in the same way as any other person. So that clarifies that they do see something and that’s why their eyes are following the image or the thing which they see and hence eyes moving while sleeping.

But then another question arises.. what do they see?? As they have not seen anything in their life so how can they imagine something, and a study claims that a person can only dream about the things which he/she has seen in the real-life, and not anything else.

I got this answer on some other page that they must see some light traveling from one place to another and hence moving eyes (Well I think they might be following a moving sound or smell). But (yes there’s a catch), if you ask a born blind person about it he/she will always deny it. This is because even if they see something they cannot define it, as they don’t know what they have seen, for them it doesn’t make any sense.

Now what I came up with was that our sub-conscious mind is very powerful and hence once a born blind person touches something feels that thing by his hand, he has a pretty good idea of what does this may look like, and hence can dream about it. Maybe in their dream, the shape won’t be perfect, but they won’t complain as they have not seen it in real. But one question still arises, how will they ever fill colors in their dreams.

Now (this is interesting trust me) one article also said that even when we are not born we see dreams in visual images (I concluded because we are attached to our mothers). So considering this why not a born blind person can see visual images in his/her dream, full of colors and full of imagination, it’s just that they won’t know or they would not be able to define this experience.
